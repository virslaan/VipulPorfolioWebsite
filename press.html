Full bio

Vipul H. Harihar, also known as Vipul Rules, is a U.S.-based computer, AI and technology scientist and engineer specializing in artificial intelligence and data science. He joined GenTrust, a New York multi-family office and registered investment adviser with nearly $6 billion under management, in 2023.

Brought in to modernize legacy code, he designed and built AIRIA, an investment operating environment for portfolio oversight, benchmarks, trading support, risk and tax workflows, and AURA, a secure enterprise AI agent platform integrated with the firm's CRM, portfolio data and documents. The platform launched in July 2025 and is now the core of the firm's trading technology. AIRIA and AURA are part of the technology foundation for VIRA, the AI-native wealth platform GenTrust and Verapath launched in September 2026. He is part of the team taking VIRA to wealth firms, including at Financial Planning's Advise AI conference in Austin.

He also wrote the firm's AI agent governance and risk review framework, covering zero data retention, personal data masking, role-based access and pre-production testing.

Before GenTrust he was an Application Developer at IBM and a research engineer at NewYork-Presbyterian and Columbia University Medical Center. He earned an MS in Data Science from Columbia University, where he was a Gold Graduate Marshal and received the Best Data Science Course Assistant award, and a BTech in Computer Science from DIT University, where he was the University Gold Medalist.
